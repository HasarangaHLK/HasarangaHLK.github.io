PROJECT 02 | 2026

🥩 Ammonia Spoilage in Meat: Kinetic Analysis

Materials / Kinetic Project

Investigated ammonia formation during meat spoilage as a potential indicator of deterioration and food quality. Studied the relationship between ammonia concentration, spoilage time, and environmental conditions by analysing the reaction kinetics of ammonia generation. Applied kinetic models and experimental data analysis to evaluate ammonia monitoring as a spoilage detection method.

Reaction Kinetics Data Analysis Materials Chemistry
