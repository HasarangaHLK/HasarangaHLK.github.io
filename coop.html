Project Overview

The Automatic Chicken Coop Door System was developed as an engineering design project to improve poultry management by reducing manual operation requirements. The system provides an automated mechanism for opening and closing a chicken coop door while considering time-based operation, environmental conditions, user safety, and reliable mechanical movement. The project combined mechanical design, electronic control, sensors, and actuator mechanisms to create a practical automation solution for poultry farming applications.

Technical Details

  • Designed a mechanical door opening and closing mechanism for automated operation.
  • Integrated sensors, control electronics, and actuator systems for system automation.
  • Implemented time-based operation to control door movement.
  • Evaluated mechanical reliability and system performance through testing.
  • Analysed safety considerations to ensure reliable user operation.

Challenges & Solutions

The main challenge was developing a reliable mechanism that could provide smooth door movement while maintaining system stability. The design required proper selection of mechanical components, actuator performance evaluation, and integration between mechanical and electronic systems. Testing and improvements were carried out to enhance reliability, accuracy, and operational performance.
