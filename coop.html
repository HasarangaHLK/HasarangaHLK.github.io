Engineering Design Project — IESE Finalist

🐔 Automatic Chicken Coop Door System

An automated poultry management solution integrating mechanical design, ESP8266-based embedded control, sensors, and actuator mechanisms to enable reliable automatic door operation.

Project Type

Engineering Design Project

Focus Area

Automation & Embedded Systems

Main Technology

ESP8266 Control

Recognition

IESE Finalist

Project Overview

The Automatic Chicken Coop Door System was developed as an engineering design project to create an automated solution for poultry management. The system reduces manual operation by enabling automatic opening and closing of a chicken coop door using embedded control, sensors, and actuator mechanisms.

The project combines mechanical design principles, electronic integration, and automation techniques to develop a practical engineering solution.

Engineering Design Approach

01. Problem Identification

Identified the requirement for an automated poultry management system to reduce manual opening and closing of the chicken coop door.

02. Concept Development

Developed a suitable automation concept by combining mechanical movement, sensing elements, and electronic control for reliable operation.

03. Mechanical Design

Designed the door mechanism considering movement, stability, structural reliability, and smooth operation.

04. Electronics Integration

Integrated ESP8266 microcontroller, sensors, and actuator mechanisms to achieve automatic door control.

05. Testing and Improvement

Evaluated system performance through testing and improved reliability, safety, and operational consistency.

System Components

  • Mechanical System: Door structure, movement mechanism, and supporting components.
  • Control System: ESP8266-based embedded controller for automation.
  • Sensing System: Sensors used for monitoring operating conditions.
  • Actuation System: Motor/actuator mechanism for opening and closing operation.
  • Power System: Electrical supply and control circuitry.

Technologies Used

ESP8266 Embedded Systems Sensors Actuator Mechanism Automation Mechanical Design Control Systems

Engineering Challenges & Solutions

The main challenge was developing a reliable mechanism that could provide smooth door movement while maintaining system stability. The design required proper integration between mechanical components and electronic control elements.

Component selection, mechanism improvement, and system testing were carried out to enhance reliability and performance.

Achievement

🏆 Finalist — Innovative Entrepreneurial Students in Engineering (IESE)


The project was recognized for developing a practical engineering solution by integrating automation, embedded control, and mechanical design concepts for poultry management.
